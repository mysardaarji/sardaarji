Our Story

A Family Table,
Open to the World

Sardaarji began in 2018 as a humble Punjabi food kiosk near Bayan Lepas, started by founder Ranjit Singh after 37 years of public service with the Penang City Council. The food struck a chord — and in 2019, the family opened Penang's first and only dedicated Punjabi restaurant, right here in George Town's Little India.

A true family venture — father, son, daughter, uncle and son-in-law — the name "Sardaarji", an honorific Punjabi title, was chosen to reflect the respect we accord every single guest. Every dish is cooked to order, never prepared ahead. Every naan is baked in a charcoal tandoor. And every guest is received the way Punjabi hospitality demands — as family.

Chef's Philosophy

Cook Slowly.
Serve Generously.

Every dish is cooked to order. Nothing prepared ahead, nothing compromised — through lockdowns and long queues alike.

Our kitchen was built by experienced chefs brought in from Punjab, and today it is led by Penang-born head chef Mohamad Ashraf, who rose from apprentice to helm one of the island’s most celebrated kitchens — keeping the cooking authentically Punjabi while nurturing young Malaysian culinary talent. The family still oversees quality on every plate that leaves the pass.
